Running

Running is my reset button. There's something meditative about the rhythm of footsteps — it's the one time my mind can be completely still or completely free to wander. I've worked my way up to a 15K as my longest distance, and the marathon is next on the list. One stride at a time.

I find a lot of parallels between running and machine learning — both reward patience, consistency, and iterating on what doesn't work. The finish line is never really the point.

Poetry

I write poems — mostly late at night, mostly about things I can't explain in code. Language has a certain precision I find beautiful; poetry is where I get to be imprecise on purpose, and somehow that feels more honest. Writing is a practice. Some poems take five minutes; some take five years. I'm still not sure there's a difference.
